## Release blocker

The installed 0.1.13 app has a running v4 terminal host. Its updater replaces the app and relaunches it, while 0.1.19 cannot authenticate and reattach to that v4 host. Publishing 0.1.19 as the latest release would offer this unsafe upgrade to the installed app. Keep the public release at 0.1.10 until the live sessions can be preserved through the transition or the user explicitly ends them. Do not quit, replace, or restart the installed app to perform release preparation.

The older v1 host also retains its documented same-user socket risk while its sessions remain active. Five v2 sessions remain running separately and have not been recovered. These risks cannot be called resolved by publishing the new ZIP.
